## Brand & Style

The brand personality is curated, generous, tactile, and celebratory—evoking the sensory intimacy of bespoke paper stationery, artisanal packaging, and ceremonial unboxing. It addresses high-intent gifters and discerning recipients seeking to bridge the emotional divide of digital gifting through deliberate ceremony, tactile depth, and artisanal finish.

The visual style blends luxury editorial minimalism with bespoke tactile physical metaphors:
- Crisp parchment textures balanced by generous negative space.
- Expressive display typography evoking heritage publishing and bespoke invitation printing.
- Physical touchstones reimagined digitally: grosgrain ribbon trims, dimensional gold wax seals, fine-gauge paper borders, and layered sleeve reveals.
- Interactions are deliberately paced to create suspense, delight, and a tangible sense of presentation rather than transactional utility.

## Colors

The palette is derived from classic heritage gift packaging and fine papeterie:

- **Canvas & Backdrops:**
  - Base Background: Tissue White (`#FFFDF8`) provides a warm, soft paper glow that avoids sterile optical glare.
  - Alternating Surfaces & Envelope Sleeves: Kraft Tan (`#D9B98B`), applied to secondary sections, card carriers, voucher sleeves, and reverse card faces.
  - Parchment Layer (Subtle Containers): `#F6EFE3` for nested modules and input surfaces requiring distinction against Tissue White.

- **Primary Accent (Ribbon Crimson - `#B3123A`):**
  - Reserved for primary calls-to-action, decorative ribbon tracks, bow accents, celebration milestones, and high-impact focal points. Text paired on this surface is always pure white (`#FFFFFF`).

- **Secondary Accent (Gift Gold - `#E8B93A`):**
  - Reserved for physical wax seal tokens, monetary amounts, denomination badges, and premium tier seals. Always carries Cocoa text (`#3A2A22`) for maximum clarity and warmth.

- **Typography & Structural Contrast (Cocoa - `#3A2A22`):**
  - Primary text, outlines, hairline delimiters, and iconography. Replaces harsh synthetic blacks with an organic, steeped umber tone.
  - Muted Copy: Cocoa at 70% opacity (`#6B584E`) for metadata, timestamps, and secondary captions.
  - Hairlines: Cocoa at 12% opacity (`rgba(58, 42, 34, 0.12)`) for deckled-edge dividers and stationery borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial grace of Playfair Display with the warm, open geometry of Plus Jakarta Sans. 

- **Playfair Display:** Carries the celebratory tone. Applied to unboxing invitations, monetary amounts within gift cards, brand declarations, and section headlines. Headlines utilize slight negative tracking to preserve optical density.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Balances the expressive serif with high legibility across redemption workflows, terms, inputs, and dense voucher parameters.
- **Micro-labels & Category Tags:** Rendered in `label-sm` or `label-md` using uppercase styling with extended letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mirror luxury blind-debossed bookbinding.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a centered 12-column grid system anchored to a maximum content width of 1240px, giving voucher cards and stationery items ample breathing room.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 40px (`2.5rem`) outer canvas margins. Unboxing stages display side-by-side: presentation sleeve on the left, recipient letter and reveal controls on the right.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) margins. Gift cards scale proportionally with stacked redemption controls.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) outer canvas margins. The gift card shifts into an immersive vertical envelope ratio with vertical pull-to-open and swipe-to-break interactions.

- **Vertical Rhythm:**
  - Alternating section backgrounds (Tissue White to Kraft Tan) feature generous vertical padding (`space-xl` scaled to 4rem to 6rem on desktop) to evoke discrete chapters of a luxury gifting catalog.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons cold tech shadows in favor of warm, diffuse ambient lighting that emulates layered stationery under warm gallery illumination:

- **Level 1 (Stationery Panels & Flat Envelopes):**
  - Shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(58, 42, 34, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(58, 42, 34, 0.04)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(58, 42, 34, 0.08)`.

- **Level 2 (Active Gift Cards & Voucher Sleeves):**
  - Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(58, 42, 34, 0.10), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(58, 42, 34, 0.06)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(217, 185, 139, 0.40)`.

- **Level 3 (Lifted Cards during Unboxing & Overlays):**
  - Shadow: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(58, 42, 34, 0.16), 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(58, 42, 34, 0.08)`.
  - Transform lift: `-4px` translation on hover or active reveal state.

- **Wax Seal Depth:**
  - Wax seals combine a multi-layered warm shadow `0 6px 14px rgba(58, 42, 34, 0.22)` with an inner bevel highlight (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60), inset 0 -2px 4px rgba(179, 18, 58, 0.25)`) to achieve organic poured-seal tactile dimensionality.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a standard roundedness of level 2 (8px base), with a dedicated `rounded-2xl` (16px) standard for all digital gift cards, vouchers, and modal envelopes:

- **Gift Cards & Voucher Carriers:** Rigidly standardized at 16px (`1rem`) border radius, preserving the tactile golden ratio of physical heavy-stock paper cards.
- **Buttons & Pill Badges:** Use full pill geometries (`rounded-full`) to complement the ribbon-and-seal metaphor.
- **Wax Seals & Coin Badges:** Elliptical, organic circular silhouettes (`rounded-full`) with micro-asymmetries (imperfections) in decorative pseudo-elements.
- **Framing & Insets:** Inner borders on cards utilize a 2px offset inset border with an 8px radius, creating a traditional debossed stationery border framing the card content.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Ribbon Crimson):** Solid `#B3123A` background, pure white text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.875rem 2rem`. Subtle inner top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). On hover: `#960E30` with slight upward translation (`-1px`).
- **Secondary (Wax Gold):** Solid `#E8B93A` background, Cocoa `#3A2A22` text, pill-shaped. Hover: `#D4A329`.
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Transparent surface, 1.5px border in `#3A2A22`, Cocoa text. On hover: background fills with `rgba(217, 185, 139, 0.20)`.

### Gift Cards & Vouchers
- **Face / Sleeve:** 16px radius, Tissue White or Kraft Tan background, enclosed in a 1px inner debossed frame (`rgba(58, 42, 34, 0.12)`). Contains denomination formatted in `display-lg` Playfair Display, recipient note in italicized body serif, and subtle blind-embossed card pattern.
- **Card Back:** Kraft Tan texture, crisp barcode/QR presentation in a clean cutout container, accompanied by expiry and balance details in tabular, high-clarity sans-serif.

### Wax Seals & Denomination Badges
- **Wax Seal Component:** Circular token in Gift Gold (`#E8B93A`) or Ribbon Crimson (`#B3123A`) with debossed monogram or crest. Functions as an interactive release latch: clicking or dragging "cracks" or lifts the seal to initiate voucher opening animations.
- **Denomination Chip:** Pill badge, Gift Gold background with Cocoa `label-md` text, anchoring the card value with high contrast.

### Ribbon Straps
- Visual anchor running vertically or horizontally along unboxing containers. Textured 24px wide ribbon stripe in Ribbon Crimson (`#B3123A`), terminating in a delicate chevron or slotted through a gold buckle/seal element.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background in warm parchment `#FAF6EE`, flat bottom border or subtle inset box shadow. 
- Inactive state: 1px border in `rgba(58, 42, 34, 0.18)`.
- Focus state: 1.5px border in Ribbon Crimson (`#B3123A`) with a gentle diffuse glow (`rgba(179, 18, 58, 0.12)`). Label typography rests in `label-sm` above the field in Cocoa 70%.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Radio buttons: Circular, Kraft Tan background with a solid Cocoa center dot upon selection.
- Checkboxes: 4px rounded squares in warm paper parchment, filled with Ribbon Crimson and a clean white check icon when active.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Delimited by fine dotted or hairline Cocoa borders (`rgba(58, 42, 34, 0.08)`).
- Metadata labels sit in uppercase `label-sm` with numeric values rendered in Playfair Display for monetary tracking.
